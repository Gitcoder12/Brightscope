# Brightscope — Company and Team Fit

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-13

## Project explanation

A research search page that lets a user look for academic papers across several open scholarly indexes from one place.

Company matches are plausible skills/employer fit only, not claims of adoption, endorsement, partnership or hiring interest.

## Google DeepMind

**Relevant public areas:** Research discovery and scientific AI tooling.

**Potential role families:** Research Engineer, Software Engineer, Data Engineer, ML Engineer or Platform Engineer.

**Evidence to build:** Browser-based research discovery UI with adapters for OpenAlex, Crossref, arXiv and other providers, metadata normalisation and deduplication. The audit found a Python Flask app incorrectly stored as .env.example, a missing templates/index.html, GitHub Pages deployment mismatch and a public-proxy fetch monkeypatch to verify.
